Step By Step Guide: New concepts for Beta Galleries are the Library & Showroom and their definitions are as follows: A Library is internal facing and it is for you to manage and store your digital assets - past campaigns, collections. Essentially a digital archive for all your digital content (videos and images). A Showroom is where you can build a beautifully curated collection of images and videos to share with your customers, press etc so they can explore more about your collection.
