How to edit multiple contacts In this article you will learn how to edit contacts in bulk Scroll to the bottom of Contact Manager and click " Export for Updates " in the lower right corner of the page. This will download an Excel document of your entire contact database or the specific contacts you have searched. NOTE: If you do not filter down your search results by Name, Category, Type, etc., you will export your entire contacts database.
