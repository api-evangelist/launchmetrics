Email Domain Manager The Email Domain Manager is where you can browse & add email domains that are verified or pending spam authentication. When you want to send an email through the Launchmetrics platform with a custom SEND FROM email address (e.g. janedoe@company.com), you must check for, and sometimes modify your authentication records in order to ensure your email is not flagged as spam.
