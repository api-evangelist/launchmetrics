Adding Invitees through Excel Upload event-specific Invitees without having to add them to your permanent Contacts database Add Invitees from Excel To add Temporary Contacts: Go into Invitees Manager for your desired event. Click “Import Invitees from Excel”. Download the Event Contacts template and fill it out.
