INVITEES EXPORT FOR UPDATES Import seating assignments, travel addresses, RSVPs and more via Excel When navigating to the bottom of the page in Invitees Manager, you can now Export for Updates. Upon clicking this button, an Excel will be downloaded with event specific information. The export includes Temporary and Permanent Contacts.
