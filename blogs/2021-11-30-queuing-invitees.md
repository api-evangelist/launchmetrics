Queuing Invitees Complete actions on multiple Invitees at once. To queue your invitees, either search for a group of invitees using the search fields and click "All Found" or use the check-box at the far left column to select the individuals you wish to queue. Queue Options Add to Contact Group: Add queued invitees to a new or existing contact group.
