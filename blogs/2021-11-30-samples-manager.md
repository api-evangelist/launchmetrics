Samples Manager The Samples Manager is a powerful tool that allows you to search, edit, and queue up samples for further action. Search for Samples : You can search for any sample detail that you enter into the system database. Simply enter the sample details you have available and click “Search.” The more information you provide, the more refined your search results will be.
