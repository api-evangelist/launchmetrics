Request the add-on Measuring the impact of your samples activation is available if you have a seat in Samples and Discover along with the necessary add-on. Contact your sales representative to activate it. Once you have confirmed a Placement on your send out, you can then tag the sample(s) that were included in the coverage in order to get more granular with your reporting.
