Tracking or monitoring Online Advertising Banners in online articles and online medias, in a similar way to print advertisements, in order to better understand sponsored content and the impact of advertising, is a request that we sometimes get from our customers. At Launchmetrics we don't currently offer this capability. While technically is relatively simple to monitor a banner this type of content has some particularities that makes it very difficult to monitor consistently.
