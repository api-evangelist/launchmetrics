Searching for content in Discover In this article, you will discover how to utilize the search bar and filters in Discover to find content. Whether you are searching for a particular link or magazine, you will find all the information you need right here! The search bar can be used to 1) find a specific URL within the online channel, or 2) search for specific content across all three channels using boolean queries.
