Introduction The new Voices tab in the Contacts section provides access to an overview of the Launchmetrics Voices list. This feature allows you to manage these Voices according to your strategic goals. With the Voice List, you will be able to: Discover new voices by: Identifying them through simple search actions and smart filtering Evaluating them using powerful, industry-standard data Validating and easily comparing them Manage your active voices by: Evaluating the performance of activated voices and comparing them with your competitors' activations.
