✨ From Coverage to Clarity 📊 Dashboards Dashboard lets you visualize your Discover data in an interactive, at-a-glance format. 👀 Instead of scrolling through your Feed or building a full report, switch to the Dashboard tab to quickly see how your coverage is performing — broken down by MIV, placement count, Voice split, geography, and more. Whether you're tracking a campaign, preparing for a debrief, or want a quick pulse check on your media coverage, Dashboard gives you a visual summary you can act on and share.
