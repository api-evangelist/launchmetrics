🗓 released June 16th ✅ Improved! Days Late in the Audit Report The Audit Report now includes a "Days Late" column showing how many days each overdue sample is past its expected return date — directly in the Scanned and Not Scanned tabs. 🎯 Why This Matters During a physical audit, teams need to act on overdue samples immediately — but the Audit Report had no way to surface how long a sample had been out past its due date.
