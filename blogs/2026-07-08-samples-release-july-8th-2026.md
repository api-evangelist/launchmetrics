🗓 released July 8th ✅ Bulk search on custom scannable fields Speed up your sample operations by pasting a whole batch of custom code and get all your samples back in a single search, turning dozens of lookups into one! You can now paste multiple values into any activated scannable field (custom barcode, external serial, external RFID, external SKU) and search them all at once, the same way the standard barcode field already worked. Click the Advanced Search button next to the field, paste your list, and the search returns any sample matching one of them.
