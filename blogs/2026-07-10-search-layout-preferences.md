Search Layout Preferences Search layout preferences allow you to customise which details are visible when you are searching for Samples or Contacts within the Launchmetrics platform. This feature enables you to tailor the information displayed according to your needs, making it easier to find and manage the data that is most relevant to you! For example, when you access the layout preferences, in Contacts you can: Select Visible Columns : You can choose which fields, such as Last Name, Associated contacts, Travel name address etc., are displayed in your search results.
