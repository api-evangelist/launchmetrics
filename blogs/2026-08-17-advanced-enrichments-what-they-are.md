Advanced AI Enrichments: what they are and what they let you analyze Standard monitoring tells you that your brand was covered. Advanced AI Enrichments tell you what that coverage was actually about: which product, which collection, which Voice, which event, and whether it was corporate news or product editorial. Same coverage, a completely different conversation.
