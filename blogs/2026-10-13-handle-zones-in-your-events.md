Introduction The new Zone Access Management feature in Events by Launchmetrics lets organizers define, control and verify who can enter restricted areas during an event, such as backstage, the riser or the red carpet. Organizers assign an access type to each invitee and staff member, front desk staff pair an RFID bracelet at check-in, and security gets an instant Admitted or Not Admitted result when they scan it at each zone entrance. General Overview Zone Access Management works across the web platform and the Events app.
